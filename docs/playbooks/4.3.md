# 4.3: Spam and duplicate apps

**Store:** Apple. **Resubmit without a new build:** only when the steps below say so.

## What it means

Apple found the app too similar to other apps on the store, often several apps from the same business or account.

## Fix

1. Check the business has only one shopper app per store, and that no other account publishes an app for the same brand.
2. Make the content brand-specific: the listing text, the screenshots and the catalog must be this business’s own.
3. Reply in Resolution Center explaining that this is the business’s single official app, sold under its own developer account.
4. Resubmit once the listing is distinct. The second 4.3 rejection always goes to a human review task.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
