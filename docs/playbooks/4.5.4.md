# 4.5.4: Push notifications

**Store:** Apple. **Resubmit without a new build:** only when the steps below say so.

## What it means

Push was used for marketing without consent, or permission was asked for at launch.

## Fix

1. Confirm push_opt_in_out passes: three switches, offers and new reels off until the shopper turns them on, and no permission request at launch.
2. Check the business’s recent offer pushes kept to the weekly cap and quiet hours.
3. Reply describing the switches, then resubmit. A new build is needed only if the app asked at launch.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
