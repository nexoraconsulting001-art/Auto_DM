# 5.1.1: Data collection and account deletion

**Store:** Apple. **Resubmit without a new build:** only when the steps below say so.

## What it means

The app collects data it does not need, its privacy details are wrong, or the shopper cannot delete their account in the app.

## Fix

1. Confirm account_deletion passes: the account screen offers Delete my account and the server answers the deletion route.
2. Delete a test account on a device: personal data must be erased and every session ended.
3. Check the privacy labels in the App listing match what the app collects and that the privacy URL opens.
4. Send a new build only if the deletion screen was missing.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
