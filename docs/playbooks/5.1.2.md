# 5.1.2: Data use and sharing

**Store:** Apple. **Resubmit without a new build:** yes, the fix is data.

## What it means

The privacy labels or the privacy policy say something different from what the app does with data.

## Fix

1. Open the App listing and check the privacy labels: no tracking, nothing shared, payment details not listed (the gateway holds them).
2. Check the business’s privacy policy page says the same.
3. Fix the labels or the policy, then resubmit the same build.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
