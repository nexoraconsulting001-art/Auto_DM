# play_data_safety: Data safety form

**Store:** Google Play. **Resubmit without a new build:** yes, the fix is data.

## What it means

The data safety answers do not match what the app does.

## Fix

1. Open the App listing for Android and compare the data safety answers with what the app collects.
2. Fix the answers in the Play Console to match, then resubmit the same build.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
