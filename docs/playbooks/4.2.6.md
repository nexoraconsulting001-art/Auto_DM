# 4.2.6: Apps made from a template

**Store:** Apple. **Resubmit without a new build:** only when the steps below say so.

## What it means

Apple saw the app as one of many generated from the same template or service.

## Fix

1. Confirm the app is submitted from the business’s own developer account (Store access shows the legal name as the seller). An app under an agency’s or a platform’s account is the usual cause.
2. Make the listing the business’s own: its own description, its own screenshots from its own catalog, its own icon and colours. Never reuse another brand’s listing text.
3. In the review notes, say who the business is, that it sells its own products, and that it publishes this app under its own name.
4. Resubmit once the listing and the account are right. No new build is needed unless the brand file itself was generic.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
