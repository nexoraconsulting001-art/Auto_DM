# 3.1.3: Payments outside in-app purchase

**Store:** Apple. **Resubmit without a new build:** only when the steps below say so.

## What it means

Apple asked why the app takes payment without in-app purchase.

## Fix

1. The app sells physical goods, which are paid through the payment gateway, not in-app purchase. Confirm gateway_payments passes in the pre-submit run: no in-app purchase library or entitlement.
2. Make sure nothing in the app sells digital content or unlocks features for money.
3. Reply that the purchases are physical goods delivered outside the app, then resubmit the same build.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
