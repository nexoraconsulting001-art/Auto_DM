# 4.8: Sign in with Apple

**Store:** Apple. **Resubmit without a new build:** only when the steps below say so.

## What it means

The app offers Google sign-in on iOS without offering Sign in with Apple as well.

## Fix

1. Check the iOS build offers Sign in with Apple (the app config sets usesAppleSignIn) and that the sign-in screen shows it.
2. Check the business’s Apple sign-in is set up and the server accepts its tokens.
3. Send a new build if the button was missing; otherwise reply and resubmit.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
