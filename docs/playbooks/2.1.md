# 2.1: App completeness and demo access

**Store:** Apple. **Resubmit without a new build:** yes, the fix is data.

## What it means

The reviewer could not sign in, hit a crash, or found a broken flow. Most often the demo number did not work.

## Fix

1. In the Platform Console, open the business and check review access is on, with the review number and its 6-digit code.
2. Sign in on a test device with that number and code, place an order with cash on delivery, and open the account screen.
3. Put the review number, the code and the steps in the App Review information of the submission.
4. This is fixed in data: resubmit the same build.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
