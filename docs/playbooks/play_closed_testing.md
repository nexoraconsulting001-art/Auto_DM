# play_closed_testing: Closed testing before production

**Store:** Google Play. **Resubmit without a new build:** only when the steps below say so.

## What it means

A personal Play account made after 13 November 2023 asked for production before its closed test finished.

## Fix

1. Open Store access for Android: the closed-testing step shows the days and testers so far.
2. The test needs at least 12 testers taking part for 14 days in a row. Ask the business to invite testers from its own customers or staff.
3. Resubmit once the step shows done. No new build is needed.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
