# 2.3: Accurate metadata

**Store:** Apple. **Resubmit without a new build:** yes, the fix is data.

## What it means

The listing promised something the app does not do, or its screenshots are not of the app.

## Fix

1. Compare the listing text with what the app does. Remove any feature the app does not have.
2. Screenshots must come from the real build with the business’s catalog (listing assets with source build_capture), never a mock-up.
3. Fix the listing in the console (App listing), then resubmit the same build.

## If it happens again

A second rejection under the same guideline (ignoring any bracket, so 4.3(a) and 4.3 count together) opens a review task in the
Platform Console and blocks the next resubmit. Do not send the app again: a person on our team reads both rejections, decides the
fix, and resolves the task with what was done. Only then can the app go back to review.
