# Review notes, with demo access

Paste into "Notes for the reviewer" (App Store Connect) and "App access" (Play Console). The review phone number and code go in the credentials fields by hand, never in this repository.

## Bhava for Business

Bhava for Business is the seller app of a platform that lets small businesses sell through their Instagram and Facebook accounts. It is free to download and has no in-app purchases; the seller's subscription is bought on the web, outside the app.

Demo access: on the sign-in screen enter the review phone number and the review code from the credentials fields. This is the review login. It opens one sample business, the demo business, and nothing else. The demo business never sends anything to real people: messages are held and shown as "would send".

What to look at: Orders (confirm a cash on delivery order), Inbox, Automations (test an automation), Catalog, Analytics. Payments keys are entered after a fresh sign-in check and are never shown again.

Permissions: the camera and photo library are used only to add product photos or a logo. Face ID or fingerprint unlocks the app on the phone. Notifications tell the seller about new orders.

Delete account: Settings, then Delete my account.

## A seller's branded app

Use the `reviewNotes` text in branded-app-template.json, with the business's own name. The review sign-in opens a demo shopper with sample orders; no real payment is taken.

## If a reviewer is rejected

The fix for each guideline is in docs/playbooks. Answer in the console the same day.
